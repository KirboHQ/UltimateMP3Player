ENGINES (separate programs the app starts)
------------------------------------------

They aren't part of Ultimate MP3 Player's code: the app runs them as programs of their own. Windows: in the "engines"
folder of the installation. Linux and macOS: not in the package, the app downloads them from their projects on its
first start. Android: inside the app (see "Android" below).

yt-dlp (Windows, Android)
  License: Unlicense (public domain), text at the end.
  Source: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp (each version is a tag there; "yt-dlp --version" shows the one you have).
  yt-dlp.exe contains Python and other libraries:
  https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/blob/master/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt

gallery-dl (Windows)
  License: GNU General Public License version 2, GPL-2.0.txt. Copyright (C) Mike Fährmann.
  Source: https://github.com/mikf/gallery-dl (each version is a tag there; "gallery-dl --version" shows the one you
  have). gallery-dl.exe contains Python and the libraries listed in its source.

FFmpeg: ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe and their DLLs (Windows)
  License: GNU General Public License version 3 (these builds include GPL libraries), LICENSE.txt.
  Built by https://github.com/yt-dlp/FFmpeg-Builds ("ffmpeg-master-latest-win64-gpl-shared").
  Source: the FFmpeg commit named by "ffmpeg -version" (N-...-g<commit>) at https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git
  (mirror: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg); the build scripts, with the exact versions of the libraries built into
